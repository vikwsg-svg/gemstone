01 / no guessing

No comps, no price

02 / evidence

Data rights

Your data stays yours.

Enterprise is separate from our consumer and dealer products at the API edge. Your valuations are isolated to your account and are never shown to other customers.

  • Tenant isolation. Every stored valuation, batch and usage record belongs to one account and is only retrievable with that account’s keys.
  • Scoped API keys. Keys are issued per product, so a Comps key cannot call anything else.
  • No training on your data by default. Your data is not used to train shared models without a signed data-contribution agreement, which earns a price discount.
  • Audit trail. Every valuation is stored with its inputs, comparables and model version and can be retrieved by ID.
