Photos in, a decision you can defend out

  1. Send photos

    One to five photos of one item, plus anything you know: metal, weight, maker, a lab report number.

  2. We scan it

    The GemstoneAI scan identifies the piece and prices it from comparable sales and listings.

  3. Your rules run

    Intake routes it and Verify scores it, using thresholds your team sets.

  4. You keep the evidence

    The decision, the comparables and the model version, stored and retrievable by ID.
