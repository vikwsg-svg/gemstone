The routing decision

Four outcomes, set by your thresholds.

You set the rules, for example “anything with an estimated value over $5,000 goes to Lab.” Every decision is logged with its reasons.

List

Low value, high confidence. Goes straight to listing with prefilled fields.

Review

A gemologist should look. The reasons are attached so the review is fast.

Lab

Value is above your threshold, or treatment or origin drives the price.

Decline

Below your platform’s floor.

Inputs

What your intake station already captures.

  • Multi-angle photos from the intake station
  • The consignor’s description
  • Paperwork: lab report, receipt or prior appraisal
  • Weight and measurements
Outputs per item

A decision your associates can act on.

  • Likely identification with alternatives and confidence, for example sapphire 82%, spinel 11%, glass 4%
  • Estimate band from Comps
  • Prefilled listing fields marked by confidence. Treatment and origin are suggested, never asserted as fact.
  • Discrepancy note when the photos contradict the consignor’s description
Delivery

At the intake station and in the back office.

  • An API for your consignment system
  • An intake-station web app for associates
  • Batch mode for back-of-house queues
Pilot success metric

Gemologist hours per 100 items, time from receipt to listing, and the share of List-routed items later corrected by an expert (target under 2%).
