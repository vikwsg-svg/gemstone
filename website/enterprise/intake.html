The routing decision

Four outcomes, set by your thresholds.

You set the rules, for example “anything with an estimated value over $5,000 goes to Lab.” Every decision is logged with its reasons.

List

Low value, high confidence. Goes straight to listing with prefilled fields.

Review

A gemologist should look. The reasons are attached so the review is fast.

Lab

Value is above your threshold, or a lab report would settle a question, such as natural vs lab-grown or treatment, that moves the price.

Decline

Below your platform’s floor.

Inputs

What your intake station already captures.

  • Multi-angle photos, including hallmarks and maker’s marks
  • The consignor’s description
  • Anything you already know: metal and weight, maker, era, a lab report number
  • Your consignment reference, echoed back on every result
Outputs per item

A decision your associates can act on.

  • Likely identification of metal and stones with alternatives and confidence, for example sapphire 82%, spinel 11%, glass 4%
  • Value range from the Scan API
  • Prefilled listing fields marked by confidence. Treatment, origin and maker attribution are suggested, never asserted as fact.
  • The reasons and the rules used, logged with the full scan, so every decision can be explained later
In the API

One call per item: the scan, then your rules.

  • Send the same photos as a scan, plus the consignor’s description and your reference.
  • Get the decision with coded reasons, the rules that produced it, suggested listing fields and the full scan behind it.
  • Change your thresholds with PUT /intake/rules; every later decision records the rules it used.
  • Work the queue with GET /intake/items, record final decisions with POST /intake/items/{id}/review, and read the pilot metrics from GET /intake/stats.

Delivery

At the intake station and in the back office.

  • The intake station: a web app for associates. Take photos on a phone or tablet, add what the consignor said, get the routing, and record the specialist’s final decision.
  • An API for your consignment system, with webhooks when each decision is ready
  • Batch mode for back-of-house queues: upload a spreadsheet of photo links, download one decision per row
  • Your rules, your thresholds, changed in the app or the API at any time
Pilot success metric

Gemologist hours per 100 items, time from receipt to listing, and the share of List-routed items later corrected by an expert (target under 2%). Specialists record their final decision on each item, and the correction rate is calculated for you as the pilot runs.
