Job to be done“Give me a defensible value for this stone in under a second, with the evidence attached.”
What comes back

Not just a number. The case for it.

  • Price band (low, mid, high) per carat and in total, in the basis you asked for.
  • The comparables used, each labeled as a realized sale or an asking price, with date, attributes and similarity score.
  • Confidence score with plain reason codes when it’s low: few comparables, missing treatment, rare origin, asking prices only.
  • Scenario pricing for unknowns, such as “if unheated” versus “if heated.”
  • Valuation ID and model version on every response, for audit and reproducibility.
Inputs

Three fields to start. Every extra one narrows the band.

  • Required: variety, carat weight, shape
  • Optional: color grade, clarity, treatment, origin, dimensions
  • Lab report: GIA, AGL, SSEF, Gübelin or GRS, with report number
Valuation basis

The value definition your process actually uses.

  • Retail replacement for insurance scheduling and claims
  • Fair market value for estates, donations and lending
  • Wholesale for trade buying and inventory
  • Auction realized for consignment and sale planning
Rules that make it enterprise-grade

What Comps will and won’t do.

5-comp floor

It refuses to guess

Fewer than five qualifying comparables means no price. You get the closest comps and “insufficient data,” and coverage is reported honestly.

valuation_id

It keeps the receipt

Every valuation is stored with its full inputs, comparables, model version and output, and can be retrieved later by ID.

model_version

It holds still when you need it to

Pin a model version per request or for your whole account, so values stay consistent across a sale or a policy term.

Delivery

Plug it into the systems you already run.

  • REST API with scoped API keys and safe retries (idempotency keys)
  • CSV batch for portfolio revaluation: upload a file, download results
  • Sandbox with synthetic stones, so your engineers can build before the pilot starts
  • Embeddable comps widget: on the roadmap
