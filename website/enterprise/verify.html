Checks

Five ways a listing or claim stops adding up.

Price anomaly

The asking price or claimed value is far outside the Comps band for the stated attributes. Too cheap usually means misdescribed; too high means inflation.

Attribute conflict

The stated treatment or origin doesn’t fit the price, or the photos don’t match the stated species or color.

Document mismatch

Lab report details such as weight, measurements and color don’t match the listing or the photos.

Duplicate imagery

The same photos appear on other listings or prior claims.

Seller pattern

Repeated anomalies across one seller’s listings (marketplaces).

Outputs

A ranked queue, with the evidence.

  • Risk score from 0 to 100
  • The checks that fired, with their evidence
  • A suggested action: allow, request lab report, manual review or hold
Delivery

Where your team already works.

  • A real-time API at listing or claim submission
  • A nightly batch over live inventory
  • Webhooks into your trust and safety or claims queue
Pilot success metric

Precision of the top 5% of flags (the share confirmed as real problems on review) and the dollar value of the problems caught.
