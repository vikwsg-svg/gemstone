Catch misdescribed jewelry before buyers do.

Verify scans the item behind a listing, claim or appraisal and scores how likely it is to be wrong, with the evidence. A risk signal for your team, not an authentication.

From score to action

0–39Allow

Or request a lab report when the identification is in doubt.

40–69Manual review

Goes to your review queue, highest risk first.

70–100Hold

Stop the listing or the payment until someone looks.

Default thresholds. Set your own in the review queue or the API.
