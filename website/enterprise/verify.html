Catch misdescribed jewelry before buyers do.

Verify scores how likely a listing, claim or appraisal is to be wrong, and says why. It is a risk signal for a person or a rules engine, never a certificate of authenticity.

Checks

Four ways a listing or claim stops adding up.

Each check reports whether it ran, whether it fired, its weight and its evidence. Weights and thresholds are yours to set.

Price anomaly

The asking price or claimed value is far outside the Scan API’s price range for the stated metal, stones and maker. Too cheap usually means misdescribed; too high means inflation.

Identification doubt

The scan can’t identify the piece, isn’t confident, or sees a likely alternative, such as a lab-grown or simulant stone.

Duplicate imagery

The same photo was already used in your account on another listing or claim.

Seller pattern

The same seller or claimant keeps getting flagged.

On the roadmap

Document checks (lab report numbers, hallmarks), field-by-field attribute conflicts, and edited or cropped copies of a photo.

Outputs

A ranked queue, with the evidence.

  • Risk score from 0 to 100
  • The checks that fired, with their evidence
  • A suggested action: allow, request lab report, manual review or hold
Delivery

Where your team already works.

  • A real-time API at listing or claim submission, available now
  • A nightly batch over live inventory and webhooks into your trust and safety or claims queue: on the roadmap
In the API

Check a listing or claim as it’s submitted.

  • Send the photos and what the listing or claim states: its id, the seller or claimant, the stated value.
  • Get a 0–100 risk score, a suggested action (allow, request lab report, manual review or hold) and every check with its evidence.
  • Set your thresholds with PUT /verify/settings. Duplicate photos and seller history are matched only within your own account.
POST /enterprise/v1/verify/checksrequest
curl https://api.gemstoneaiapp.com/enterprise/v1/verify/checks \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer gsk_test_…" \
  -d '{"photos": [{"url": "https://…/claim-photo-1.jpg"}],
       "subject": {"type": "claim",
         "external_id": "CLM-5512",
         "seller_id": "policyholder-8812",
         "stated_value": 14500}}'
