05IndexEarly access

The price index for jewelry and gemstones.

Index licenses the GemstoneAI Price Index (GPI) and the category data beneath it, for people who need to know where prices are moving without integrating an API.

Request a pilot Ask for a sample report
Job to be done“Tell me where jewelry and gemstone prices are moving, by category, maker, period, species, origin and size, so I can plan sales, set price policies or lend against inventory.”
What’s licensed

From the headline number down to the cell.

  • GPI headline index plus sub-indices by category: natural and lab-grown diamonds, colored gemstones, signed jewelry, period jewelry, gold and platinum pieces. Species, origin, size band and treatment status to follow.
  • Monthly price series with methodology notes and comparable counts on every value; thin values are flagged, not hidden
  • Quarterly market report, co-brandable for your client communications, on request
  • Raw comparable access under a stricter license, on request
Credibility

An index you can cite.

  • A published methodology, versioned on every value
  • A fixed rebalancing calendar
  • Published values are never overwritten. Corrections are logged with the old value, the new value and the reason.
  • One calculation and one source of truth: the feed serves exactly what the GPI publishes
Delivery

Read it, pull it or present it.

  • An API or CSV feed, available now
  • A PDF quarterly report, on request
  • A dashboard: on the roadmap
Who licenses it

Beyond the trade floor.

  • Auction houses and insurers planning sales and price policies
  • Banks, pawn and specialty lenders that take jewelry as collateral
  • Wealth managers with private-client collections
  • Trade press and research
In the API

Monthly levels, with the evidence count on every value.

  • GET /index/series lists every series with its latest value.
  • GET /index/series/{key} returns monthly levels with comparable counts, a thin-data flag, month-on-month and year-on-year change, and whether a value was revised. JSON or CSV.
  • GET /index/corrections lists every correction with the old and new value and the reason.
  • Sandbox keys return example series, so you can build before you license.
GET /enterprise/v1/index/series/gpirequest
