03EstimateEarly access

Documented estimates for every jewelry lot, backed by realized results.

Estimate gives specialists a documented starting point for pre-sale estimates and reserves. It supports their judgment rather than replacing it.

Request a pilot Built on Comps
Job to be done“Draft estimates for the 300 lower-value jewelry lots in this online sale, and show me the realized results that justify each one.”
Inputs

What your catalogue already holds.

  • Lot description and catalogue fields: metal, stones, maker, period
  • Lab report data
  • Photos
  • Sale context: house, venue, live or online, season
Outputs per lot

An estimate a specialist can sign.

  • Suggested low and high estimate, rounded to auction increments
  • Suggested reserve as a percentage of the low estimate, using your house policy
  • Realized comps first. Asking-price comps are clearly separated and weighted lower.
  • Sell-through risk: how comparable lots performed against estimate
  • A one-paragraph rationale to edit into a condition report or consignor letter
Where it works

Built for the lots that stretch your department.

Online and mid-tier sales with $1,000–$50,000 lots, where specialists are stretched and comparables are dense.

Top lots, such as important signed pieces, rare origins and large unheated stones, get comparables and context only, with no number, unless at least five qualifying comparables exist.

Consignor-facing variant

A white-label valuation page for prospective consignors.

Send prospects a branded “free auction estimate for your jewelry” page that feeds your lead pipeline.

Pilot success metric

The share of hammer prices that fall inside the suggested estimate band, back-tested on your house’s past sales and compared with your own published estimates.

Back-test Estimate on your last sale.

Estimate is in early access. We start by back-testing against your published estimates, so you see the result before your specialists change anything.
