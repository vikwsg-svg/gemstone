03EstimateEarly access

Documented estimates for every jewelry lot, backed by realized results.

Estimate gives specialists a documented starting point for pre-sale estimates and reserves. It supports their judgment rather than replacing it.

Request a pilot Built on the Scan API
Job to be done“Draft estimates for the 300 lower-value jewelry lots in this online sale, and show me the realized results that justify each one.”
Inputs

What your catalogue already holds.

  • Photos of the lot
  • Catalogue fields you already have: metal, stones, maker, period, a lab report number
  • Sale context: house, sale, live or online, date
  • The lot number, echoed back on every result
Outputs per lot

An estimate a specialist can sign.

  • Suggested low and high estimate, rounded to auction increments
  • Suggested reserve as a percentage of the low estimate, using your house policy
  • Built only from realized sales, the middle half of at least five. Asking prices are listed separately and never used for the number.
  • A one-paragraph rationale to edit into a condition report or consignor letter
  • Sell-through risk against past estimates: on the roadmap
Where it works

Built for the lots that stretch your department.

Online and mid-tier sales with $1,000–$50,000 lots, where specialists are stretched and comparables are dense.

Top lots, such as important signed pieces, rare origins and large unheated stones, get comparables and context only, with no number, unless at least five realized sales exist.

Consignor-facing variant · on the roadmap

A white-label valuation page for prospective consignors.

Send prospects a branded “free auction estimate for your jewelry” page that feeds your lead pipeline.

In the API

A starting point a specialist can sign, with the sales behind it.

  • Send the lot’s photos and catalogue fields; the scan finds the comparables.
  • With five or more realized sales you get a low and high estimate rounded to auction increments, the sales used and an editable rationale.
  • With fewer you get no_estimate and the reason, with the comparables for reference. No number without realized evidence.
  • Set your reserve policy and increments with PUT /estimate/policy.
POST /enterprise/v1/estimate/lotsrequest
curl https://api.gemstoneaiapp.com/enterprise/v1/estimate/lots \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer gsk_test_…" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: feb-sale-lot-214" \
  -d '{"photos": [{"url": "https://…/lot-214.jpg"}],
       "details": {"item_type": "ring",
         "metal": {"type": "platinum"}},
       "lot_number": "214",
       "sale": {"sale_name": "Fine Jewels Online",
                "venue": "online"}}'
