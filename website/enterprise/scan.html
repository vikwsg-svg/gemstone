Job to be done“Tell me what this piece is and what it’s worth, with the evidence attached.”
What comes back

The whole scan result, in one call.

  • Identification from the photos: what it is, how sure the scan is, what was observed, what else it could be, what’s still open and the evidence that would settle it.
  • Price: a range and what kind of value it is, or the reason no price is given.
  • The comparables behind the price, each labeled as a sale or an asking price, with source, date, link and how it differs from your item.
  • Scan ID and model version on every response, for audit and reproducibility.
Inputs

Photos, plus anything you already know.

  • Photos: 1 to 5 of the same item: front, side, and any stamps inside the band or on the clasp
  • Details you already know, optional: item type, metal and weight, maker and signature, era, condition, a lab report number, or free-text notes from a catalogue or appraisal
  • Your own reference, such as a policy or lot number, echoed back on every result
Built for business

What the API adds to the scan.

  • One call returns the identification, price and comparables together
  • Every scan stored and retrievable by ID, for audit
  • Safe retries, usage counts and spreadsheet batch uploads
  • Version stamp on every result, with an optional pin
Rules that make it enterprise-grade

What the Scan API will and won’t do.

no guessing

It refuses to guess

An item it can’t identify, or fewer than five comparables, means no price. You get the reason, what it found and the evidence that would settle it.

scan_id

It keeps the receipt

Every scan is stored with its inputs, identification, comparables, model version and output, and can be retrieved later by ID.

model_version

It tells you which model answered

Every result is stamped with the scan model that produced it. Pin one for your account to keep results consistent across a sale or a policy term.

Delivery

Plug it into the systems you already run.

  • REST API with scoped API keys and safe retries (idempotency keys)
  • CSV batch for portfolio revaluation and intake queues: photo links and details per row, a result per item
  • Sandbox with example scan results, so your engineers can build before the pilot starts
  • Embeddable scan widget: on the roadmap
