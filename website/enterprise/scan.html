What comes back

What it is

The identification, how sure the scan is, what else it could be, and the evidence that would settle it.

What it’s worth

A price range and the kind of value it is, such as retail replacement or resale. Or the reason there is no price.

Why

The comparables behind the price, each labelled sold or asking, with source, date and how it differs from your item.

Every response carries a scan ID and the model version, and is stored for audit.

Rules it keeps

Five comparables or no price

Below five, the price is withheld with the reason. The comparables it found are still returned.

Sold and asking kept apart

Auction and resale teams judge by realized prices, so every comparable says which it is.

Pin a model version

Keep results consistent across a policy term or a sale. Every result names the model that produced it.

Plug it into what you run

REST API

Scoped API keys and safe retries. Slow scans answer at once and finish on retrieval.

Spreadsheet batch

Revalue a portfolio: one row of photo links per item in, one result per row out.
