Job to be done“Tell me what this piece is and what it’s worth, with the evidence attached.”
What comes back

Not just a number. The case for it.

  • Identification from the photos: what it is, what was observed, what else it could be, what’s still open and the evidence that would settle it.
  • Price range (low, mid, high) in the basis you asked for, and per carat for loose stones.
  • Raw / Make / Market breakdown for finished pieces: materials, craftsmanship and the brand or period premium.
  • The comparables used, each labeled as a realized sale or an asking price, with date, attributes, similarity and how it differs from your item.
  • Confidence score with plain reason codes when it’s low: few comparables, natural vs lab-grown not stated, unconfirmed maker, asking prices only.
  • Scenario pricing for unknowns, such as “if natural” versus “if lab-grown,” or “if unheated” versus “if heated.”
  • Scan ID and model version on every response, for audit and reproducibility.
Inputs

Photos, details, or both.

  • Photos: 1 to 5 of the same item: front, side, and any stamps inside the band or on the clasp
  • Details you already know always win over what the photos suggest: item type, metal and weight, center and side stones, natural or lab-grown, treatment, origin, maker and signature, era, condition
  • No photos? Send the details alone, from a catalogue or an intake form
  • Lab report: GIA, IGI, HRD, AGL, SSEF, Gübelin or GRS, with report number
Valuation basis

The value definition your process actually uses.

  • Retail replacement for insurance scheduling and claims
  • Fair market value for estates, donations and lending
  • Wholesale for trade buying and inventory
  • Auction realized for consignment and sale planning
Rules that make it enterprise-grade

What the Scan API will and won’t do.

no guessing

It refuses to guess

An item it can’t identify well enough, or fewer than five qualifying comparables, means no price. You get what it found and the evidence that would settle it, and coverage is reported honestly.

scan_id

It keeps the receipt

Every scan is stored with its inputs, identification, comparables, model version and output, and can be retrieved later by ID.

model_version

It holds still when you need it to

Pin a model version per request or for your whole account, so values stay consistent across a sale or a policy term.

Delivery

Plug it into the systems you already run.

  • REST API with scoped API keys and safe retries (idempotency keys)
  • CSV batch for portfolio revaluation and intake queues: photo links and details per row, a result per item
  • Sandbox with simulated identification and synthetic comparables, so your engineers can build before the pilot starts
  • Embeddable scan widget: on the roadmap
