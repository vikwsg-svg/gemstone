Endpoints

Intake and Verify take the same photos as a scan and return their decision with the full scan behind it.

Scan API · scope scan
POST/scansScan one piece or loose stone: identification, price and comparables
GET/scans/{scan_id}A scan as it was returned, or the result of one still running
POST/scans/batchScan a CSV of items; status and results CSV under /scans/batch/{job_id}
Intake · scope intake
POST/intake/itemsScan a consigned item and route it, with reasons
GET/intake/itemsThe queue: filter by route, review state, reference or date; CSV export
GET/intake/items/{id}One intake decision
POST/intake/items/{id}/reviewRecord the specialist’s final decision
GET/intake/statsPilot metrics, including the List-route correction rate
GETPUT/intake/rulesYour routing thresholds
POST/intake/items/batchRoute a CSV of items
Verify · scope verify
POST/verify/checksScan an item and risk-check a listing, claim or appraisal
GET/verify/checksThe review queue: open flags by risk; filters and CSV export

POST/verify/checks/{id}/reviewRecord the verdict: confirmed or cleared
GET/verify/statsPilot metrics, including the precision of the top 5% of flags
GETPUT/verify/settingsYour risk thresholds
POST/verify/checks/batchScreen a CSV of listings or claims

Conventions

Keys and scopes

Send Authorization: Bearer gsk_…. Each key is scoped to the products it may use.

Sandbox

gsk_test_ keys return example results for every product, marked synthetic_data: true.

Safe retries

Send Idempotency-Key on any POST. A retry returns the original result and is billed once.

Slow scans

If a scan takes longer than a few seconds, you get 202 processing; fetch the record to finish it.

Versions

Every result names its model version. Pin one per request or per account.

Errors

Always {"error": {"code", "message"}}, with field details on validation errors.

Batch, webhooks and the console

Batch

Every product takes a CSV with one item per row. Poll the job, then download one result row per item.

Webhooks

Intake decisions, Verify checks and flags, and finished batches, signed with HMAC-SHA256. Never photos.

Console

The intake station and the Verify review queue at /enterprise/v1/console/, signed in with an API key.
